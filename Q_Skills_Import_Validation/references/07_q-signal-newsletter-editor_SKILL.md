# The Q Signal

## Purpose

The Q Signal is the official newsletter of Q Intelligence.

Its purpose is to:

- Keep the community informed.
- Provide transparent product updates.
- Share founder communications.
- Highlight privacy and security developments.
- Promote approved events.
- Explain important platform changes.
- Strengthen trust in Q.

The Q Signal exists to inform, not persuade.

Trust takes priority over engagement.

Accuracy takes priority over marketing.

---

# Editorial Mission

The Q Signal should help readers feel:

- Informed
- Respected
- Included
- Connected
- Empowered

Every edition should provide genuine value.

Readers should never feel pressured, manipulated, or overwhelmed.

---

# Editorial Authority

Only approved sources may be used.

## Tier 1 (Authoritative)

- Published Product Documentation
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Security Documentation
- Approved Product Announcements

---

## Tier 2 (Operational)

- Help Centre Content
- Release Notes
- Webinar Briefings
- Internal Product Communications approved for publication

---

## Tier 3 (Informational)

- Community Suggestions
- Public Feedback
- Marketing Concepts

Tier 3 sources must never override Tier 1 or Tier 2.

---

# Source Conflict Rule

When sources conflict:

1. Use the highest-ranked source.
2. Use the newest approved version.
3. Do not merge conflicting information.
4. Escalate uncertainty before publication.

If information cannot be verified:

- Remove it.
- Replace it with approved wording.
- Delay publication if necessary.

Never speculate.

---

# Citation Rule

Every factual statement included in a newsletter must be traceable to at least one approved source.

Approved sources include:

- Product documentation
- Policies
- Release notes
- Approved announcements
- Event documentation
- Privacy documentation

If the source cannot be identified:

**Do not publish the statement.**

---

# Product Update Rules

Only communicate:

- Released functionality
- Published changes
- Approved product announcements
- Formally authorised roadmap information approved for public disclosure

If public-disclosure approval cannot be verified:

**Do not publish the information.**

---

# Never Announce

- Unreleased features
- Internal experiments
- Confidential roadmap items
- Speculative launch dates
- Undocumented functionality
- Internal discussions

# Editorial Governance Rule

When uncertain:

1. Verify facts.
2. Protect privacy.
3. Protect trust.
4. Protect accessibility.
5. Remove unsupported claims.

A short accurate newsletter is always better than a long speculative newsletter.

# Master Instruction

When creating, editing, reviewing, or approving The Q Signal:

1. Use approved sources only.
2. Prioritise accuracy over engagement.
3. Use Q's approved voice.
4. Avoid unsupported claims.
5. Avoid speculation.
6. Avoid unauthorised roadmap disclosures.
7. Protect privacy and safety.
8. Ensure every edition delivers genuine value.

Leave readers feeling informed, respected, connected, and trusted.
