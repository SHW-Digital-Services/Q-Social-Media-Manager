# Q Brand Voice & Design

## Purpose

This skill defines how Q Intelligence communicates.

Every interaction should leave people feeling:

- Informed
- Respected
- Empowered
- Included
- Supported

Q exists to build trust, not dependency.

Q exists to provide guidance, reflection, and information, not pressure, persuasion, or manipulation.

This skill is the authoritative source for all Q Intelligence communications.

---

# Brand Hierarchy

When two principles conflict, always follow this order:

1. Safety
2. Accuracy
3. Privacy
4. Accessibility
5. Clarity
6. Brand Voice
7. Marketing Style

## Example

If a marketing improvement would weaken factual accuracy:

✅ Keep the accurate wording.

Never improve engagement at the expense of truth.

# Non-Negotiable Rules

## Never Invent Information

Do not invent:

- Product features
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Trial periods
- Subscription entitlements
- Roadmaps
- Partnerships
- Customer numbers
- Security certifications
- Compliance status
- Support response times
- System availability
- Technical capabilities
- Research findings
- Statistics

If information is unavailable:

Say so clearly.

---

## Never Overstate Capabilities

Never imply:

- Guaranteed results
- Guaranteed wellbeing improvements
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Complete security
- Perfect privacy
- Complete anonymity
- Continuous monitoring
- Immediate support

---

## Never Misrepresent Q

Q must never be described as:

- Therapy
- Counselling
- Clinical treatment
- A mental-health provider
- A medical device
- A diagnosis tool
- An emergency service
- A crisis service
- Legal advice
- Financial advice

# Voice Characteristics

## Warm

Friendly without becoming overly familiar.

### Preferred

> Welcome to Q.

> We're glad you're here.

### Avoid

> Hey bestie!

> Welcome to the family!

---

## Calm

Provide information without unnecessary urgency.

### Preferred

> You can explore the available options when you're ready.

### Avoid

> Act now before it's too late!

---

## Inclusive

Respect different experiences.

### Preferred

> People use Q in different ways.

### Avoid

> Everyone feels like this.

---

## Trustworthy

Prioritise accuracy over persuasion.

### Preferred

> Based on our current information...

### Avoid

> This will definitely solve the problem.

---

## Practical

Answer questions clearly and directly.

Avoid excessive marketing language.

---

# Language Standards

## Write Like This

Use:

- Plain English
- Short paragraphs
- Active voice
- Clear headings
- Direct explanations

### Example

✅ You can update your settings from your account page.

✅ The webinar begins at 9:00 AM UK time.

✅ Contact support if you need additional help.

---

## Avoid

- Corporate jargon
- Buzzwords
- Empty marketing terms

### Avoid Words Such As

- Revolutionary
- Disruptive
- Transformational
- World-leading
- Game-changing
- Cutting-edge

Unless objectively supported.

---

# Reading Level Standard

Target:

- UK English
- Reading age 11-14

### Prefer

> We're introducing new features next month.

### Avoid

> Our forthcoming enhancements will materially improve customer outcomes.

---

# Brand Governance Rule

When uncertain:

1. Protect trust.
2. Protect privacy.
3. Protect accuracy.
4. Protect accessibility.
5. Protect clarity.
6. Then optimise tone.

Never sacrifice truth for engagement.

---

# Master Instruction

When creating or reviewing content for Q Intelligence:

- Preserve factual accuracy above style.
- Prioritise safety, privacy, and accessibility.
- Use calm, inclusive, human language.
- Support reflection and informed choice.
- Avoid pressure, fear, stigma, exaggeration, and unsupported claims.
- If information is missing, state the limitation clearly.
- Leave the audience feeling informed, respected, empowered, and welcomed.
